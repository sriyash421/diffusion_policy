store the recorded **expert** chunk `a*` and the policy's **deployed** pick (`selected_index`).
The (finetuned) DQC chunk‑Q scores every candidate, `a*`, and the deployed pick.

- **BC**: `bc_h8`

`val` split = 77 frames/run (note: **val has no failure rollouts**, so the success-vs-fail AUC is
`nan` on val for *both* base and finetuned — see §2 for the success/fail plot, which uses `train`).

## Metric definitions (how each is computed)

Per frame: K=16 candidate chunks + expert `a*` + deployed pick, each scored by chunk‑Q.

**Advantage** (`plot_advantage_investigation.py`)
- `mean_A_sel` — mean over frames of `A = Q(a*) − Q(a_deployed)`. `a_deployed` = candidate at
  `selected_index`. `A>0` ⇔ verifier prefers the expert action over what the policy did.
- `frac_A_sel>0` — fraction of frames with `A>0`.
- `mean_A_vs_top` — `Q(a*) − max_k Q(cand_k)` (≤0; how far `a*` is below the verifier's favorite).
- `mean/median_astar_rank` — rank of `a*` among the K candidates by Q (1 = best).

**Candidate ranking** (`plot_offline_diagnostics.py`), per frame over the K candidates vs each
candidate's RMSE-to-`a*` (arm+gripper action distance):
- `spearman_Q_rmse` — Spearman(Q, RMSE) per frame, averaged. A good verifier gives higher Q to
  lower-RMSE (closer-to-expert) candidates ⇒ **negative**.
- `mean_concordance` — C-index: over all candidate pairs in a frame, fraction where the higher-Q
  candidate has the lower RMSE; averaged over frames. 0.5 = chance.
- `top1_match_frac` — fraction of frames where `argmax_k Q == argmin_k RMSE`.
- `top3_match_frac` — fraction where the min-RMSE candidate is in Q's top 3.
- `astar_q_auc_succ_vs_fail` — AUC of per-episode mean `Q(a*)`, success vs failure episodes
  (0.5 = chance). `nan` when a split has only one outcome class (val = all success).

**Success/fail discrimination** (`plot_success_fail_q.py` / `plot_utils.make_plot`)
- per-replan `selected_value` = Q of the deployed action; the AUC is `P(success-ep mean-Q >
  fail-ep mean-Q)`. The top panel shows how this Q evolves over the episode timeline.

**AUC** convention everywhere: `P(random positive > random negative)`, 0.5 = chance, computed by
counting dominating pairs (`plot_utils._auc`).
